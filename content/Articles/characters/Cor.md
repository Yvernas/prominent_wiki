High Commander Cor was first met by the party during [[session 3]], after which he travelled with the party to the castle and assisted them with breaking into the castle. It was revealed that [[the clone king]] had fired him from his post and gave it instead to [[Raviente]]. After [[King Hayloft Princis]] was saved Cor was reinstated to the position of high commander. After the banquet he would bring [[The Party]] to the [[Barovia |Barovian]] border. He fights using 2 massive golden greatsword, each of which have the name of one of his mothers engraved into them in orcish, those names being Koume and Kotake.
