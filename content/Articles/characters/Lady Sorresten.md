Sister of [[Lady Addax]], Lady Sorresten is a fire elemental from [[The Everburning Empire]] 
and was send as a delegate to the banquet. She is more timid than her sister but, the day after the banquet, she gifted [[The Party]] a full bar of silver, the most valuable metal in their civilization.
