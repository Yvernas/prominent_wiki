Sister of [[Lady Sorresten]], she is a fire elemental from [[The Everburning Empire]] and was send as a delegate to the banquet. Here she talked to [[Argai]] before revealing to everyone at the banquet the attrocities commited by [[The Dragonborn Federation]] to those of lower class who live within its border. She would later warm up to [[Morgan]] and [[Ovrur]] after the fight with [[Emily Thorne]].
