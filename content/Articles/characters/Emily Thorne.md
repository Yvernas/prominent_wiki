The ruler of [[Barovia]] and the wife of [[Emily's Wife]], Emily Thorne is a tall and relatively slender woman with medium length black hair usualy tied back, and she has a sharp face. She has shown to be a powerful spellcaster and confirmably has at least 1 of [[The devil shards]], and has shown the ability to summon [[Lord Girahim]] and [[The black dragon]]. She has been confirmed to be the person behind the kidnapping and replacing of [[King Hayloft Princis]] and has the soul of [[Jack Goldwyn]] imprisoned in a small d8-shaped gem.
