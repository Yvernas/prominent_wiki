After getting soem farewell gifts from [[Morgan]], [[Lady Addax]] and [[Lady Sorresten]], the party went on a little shopping spree, gathering rations, healing potions and some runes. 
The party then were then brought by [[Cor]] over the span of two days to the [[Barovia |Barovian]] border. Here they would, after Ace heard a voice in his head saying: "Watch out, they're close", enter into a church, where they found [[Emily Thorne]] praying before a set of stained glass windows depicting [[the seven]]. [[Ace Goldwyn]] rushed over to try and strike at Emily and hit but was teleported away by an arcane trap. She turned around and declared she had been waiting to introduce someone to the party. Clouds covered the sky as no more light seeped through the windows leaving only the candlelight and darkness as out of the fresco painting on the ceiling depicting [[The First Battle]] crawled slowly a figure, first covered in flecks of paint, then blood, and as she landed on the ground revealing herself to be covered in a mix of formal attire and armor. A strong, bulky, vampiric woman, [[Emily's wife]] appeared. Who within a split second dashed towards Ace and tried to stab him before attempting to dominate him. A short battle insued which ended with Ace dying and Emily muzzled before her wife dashed to her and teleported away. Ace was quickly healed by [[Ovrur]]
