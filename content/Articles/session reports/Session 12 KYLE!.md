 After deciding to return to [[Midgar]] witth [[Prompto]], the party figured out that Midgar was having some supply issues, so they decided to go to [[Thaymore]] to gather supplies and potentially find an artifact. After fighting and escaping some Werewolves they arrived at Thaymore which seemed abandoned. Under [[Akshay]]'s instruction [[Ace Goldwyn]] would smash in a window to steal a healing potion and then got shot by some hooded assassin. After talking and argument they figured out that Thaymore wasn't abandoned but everyone was hiding and they managed to convince the assassin to help them bring supplies to Midgar if Midgar promised to help Thaymore in their hour of need. The party then figured out that the assassin is a gobling called [[Kyle]] and that he wields an artifact called [[The Condemner]] and that he knew of an artifact wielder that recently died in Barovia.
