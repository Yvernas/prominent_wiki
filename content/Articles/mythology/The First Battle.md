The First Battle started before time had a name, back when the world was empty and lasted longer than the life of a god. It was the battle between [[The two of one]], who each wielded blades the size of the sky. The sparks that flew of their interlocking blades where the only thing illuminating the world at the time. After time had lost count of itself something happened. The two blades clashed as they had an infinite number of times before, but this time they shattered. Shattered into millions of grains of bright golden sand, and with that, time gained a name. This sand of creation slowly formed an orb. This orb of sand would eventually become the world we walk on today. The two of one looked at this world and embraced eachother, swearing to never pick up arms again.
