Firblod is the name of the compound derived from dragonblood. It is rare, highly illegal and extremely potent.

Some of its known effects/uses include:
Fast and powerfull healing.
The spontanous awakening of draconic sorcery.
An extreme boost to strength.
A highly powerful spell component.

A bottle of it was in the possession of the [[Cape guy]] and was stolen by [[The fourfold demon]] before it was obtained by [[The Party]].
