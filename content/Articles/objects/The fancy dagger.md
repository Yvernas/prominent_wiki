 The fancy dagger (named and wielded by [[Ace Goldwyn]]) is a silver dagger with multiple sharply cut red crystals and is known for it's ability to manipulate blood.
 
 It was created when [[Ace Goldwyn]] was in [[Cinders lair]], rummaging through the hoard where he found a glass decorated with silver containing a blood-like substance. Ace dipped one of his rusty daggers into the blood and the glass shattered and the blood consumed the rusty daggers and transformed it into The fancy dagger.

The dagger can, after a successfull attack, consume 1 action to pull the blood out of the wound created by the attack, dealing 1d6 damage. It can also pull the blood from the wielder instead and fire crystalized shards of blood into the target, dealing 1d4 damage to the wielder and 2d6 damage to the target.
